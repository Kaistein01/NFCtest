The data being encoded is a ruler of 6-character chunks NNNNN. where NNNNN is the running byte count at that chunk's end. Base64 maps 4 characters onto 3 bytes, so a truncated fragment still decodes cleanly up to its last whole group — and the tail of the decoded ruler then states its own surviving length.
