Three payloads with known-correct decodes, for checking the page against tldecode.ps1 without a board. This page decodes only — it has no encoder, so real payloads always come from the logger.

Generate a ruler URL (fragment length probe)

—

The ruler is 6-character chunks NNNNN. where NNNNN is the running byte count at that chunk's end. Base64 maps 4 characters onto 3 bytes, so a truncated fragment still decodes cleanly up to its last whole group — and the tail of the decoded ruler then states its own surviving length. That self-describing property is why the ruler is kept: a tempcomp payload cannot do it.

Self-test

—

The decoder is checked against an embedded 70-sample vector and its known output, in this browser, on load. The vector exercises every code path: small deltas, a 32-sample zero-delta run, two faults, both clamp limits, a jump wide enough to force the escape option, and a negative ramp.
